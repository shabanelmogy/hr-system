# Decision log

Permanent IDs. Never edit the meaning of an accepted decision — add a new one that supersedes it.

Template:

```markdown
### D-000 — <title>

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by D-###
- **Decided by:** <owner name/role>
- **Context:** why a decision was needed (link Q-###, BR-###, R-###)
- **Decision:** what was decided
- **Consequences:** what this makes easier/harder; follow-up work
```
